# Marine background sampling

Read the [modelling rule](../../rules/modelling-and-evaluation.md) and [sampling reference](references/sampling-design.md). Use when the comparison sample or target interpretation changes.

1. Confirm actual occurrence evidence, study domain, row meaning and whether genuine absence observations exist. Do not manufacture absences from missing records.
2. Compare sampling-domain and bias-handling options against ecology, coverage and intended prediction use. Distinguish background from pseudo-absence assumptions.
3. Specify the target labels, sample origin, overlap/exclusion policy, repeated-cell treatment, sample counts and randomisation configuration.
4. Coordinate sampling with validation partitions so construction does not exploit held-out outcomes. Plan sensitivity checks for material alternatives.
5. Record the evidence and rationale in a dated [evaluation plan](../../../docs/templates/validation-and-evaluation-plan.md) and [decision record](../../../docs/templates/decision-record.md); update the [register](../../../docs/project/decision-register.md).

Return a defensible design and interpretation limits. Guidance preparation does not authorise dataset construction or select a final strategy.
