# IT3091 evidence

Read the [evidence rule](../../rules/assignment-evidence.md), [requirements](../../../PROJECT_REQUIREMENTS.md) and [current status](../../../docs/project/repository-readiness-and-alignment.md).

1. Map each requirement and claim to an actual dataset, analysis artefact, decision or approval record. Distinguish planning support from completed evidence.
2. Copy the appropriate [template](../../../docs/templates/README.md) into `docs/records/`, following the [record conventions](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#project-evidence-records), linking dataset versions and generating artefacts. Keep originals blank.
3. Connect the comparison/evaluation to practical recommendations, uncertainty and domain limits. Preserve baseline-plus-three-alternative and justified-validation requirements.
4. Check group and individual deliverables against the assessment checklist and [responsibility overview and linked detailed files](../../../docs/project/member-responsibilities.md). Cover every proposed role and shared duty; keep planned ownership and factual activity logs separate from completed contribution evidence and assessed individual learning reports.
5. Record actual AI assistance and verification in the acting contributor's log under the [recording rule](../../rules/ai-usage.md), using the [AI-use form](../../../docs/project/ai-usage-log-template.md). Report approval, engagement and bonus evidence exactly as established.
6. Update readiness and remaining gaps in the status review; do not estimate marks or imply a template satisfies an evidence requirement.

Return traceable evidence or a concise gap review. This workflow organises assessment material; it does not manufacture underlying data or model results.
