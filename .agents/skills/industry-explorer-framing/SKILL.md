# Industry Explorer framing

Read the [scope rule](../../rules/project-scope.md) and [requirements](../../../PROJECT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

1. Establish the stakeholder or verifiable context, decision need, proposed output and realistic value. Keep Marine Resilience central and justify any Coastal Tourism connection.
2. Bound species, region and analytical ambition against feasibility evidence. Describe row meaning, output, candidate sources and exclusions without treating them as final choices prematurely.
3. Separate group agreement, proposal submission and documented track approval. Capture actual approval details and unresolved evidence; do not invent engagement or commitments.
4. Copy the [framing form](../../../docs/templates/problem-framing-canvas.md) into `docs/records/`, following the [record conventions](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#project-evidence-records) when authorised, then update the [decision register](../../../docs/project/decision-register.md).

Return a concise framing statement, feasible scope rationale and approval/context gaps. Apply the bonus's seven-week condition without inferring calendar deadlines.
