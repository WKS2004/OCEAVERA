# Scope and Industry Explorer rules

Apply when changing framing, stakeholder context, lenses, scope or track claims. Consult the [overview and scope](../../docs/project/project-overview-and-scope.md) and [assessment map](../../PROJECT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

- Preserve OCEAVERA as the proposed marine intelligence component of BLUEVERSE, centred on species distribution and habitat suitability.
- Marine Resilience is the selected primary lens. Coastal Tourism is supporting context and must strengthen the primary decision rather than create another ML task.
- Industry Explorer is selected, but prior approval must be evidenced. An agreed group working scope is not proof of lecturer approval. Record approver, date, approved scope and verifiable evidence when available.
- Identify an actual stakeholder or verifiable context, decision need and realistic value before claiming engagement. The bonus scope must be manageable within seven weeks; do not invent calendar dates or bonus entitlement.
- Keep species, marine boundary and implementation choices open until evidence supports them and the decision register is updated. Sri Lanka is a preference, not a final boundary.
- Real-time deployment, future-climate projection, full BLUEVERSE implementation and additional marine models are outside the proposed assignment scope.
- Retain the shared technical-participation principle. The [member plan](../../docs/project/member-responsibilities.md) preserves the proposal's complete allocation at the user's explicit request. D-028 finalises the current working contribution plan; role changes require recorded evidence and authorisation; retain shared scientific decisions and record authorised allocation changes. Do not infer completed contributions from ownership. Factual per-contributor repository activity is recorded under the [AI-usage rule](ai-usage.md).
