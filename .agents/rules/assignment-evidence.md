# Assignment evidence rules

Apply when preparing logs, comparisons, reports, recommendations or submission material. Use the [assessment map](../../PROJECT_REQUIREMENTS.md) as the requirement authority and [status review](../../docs/project/repository-readiness-and-alignment.md) for present evidence.

- Maintain all seven core evidence types: framing canvas, workflow diagram, decision log, EDA insights, preprocessing/features, model/method comparison, and recommendation with limitations.
- Tie claims to actual dataset versions, generating artefacts and recorded decisions. Copy [templates](../../docs/templates/README.md) into dated [records](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#project-evidence-records); keep originals blank.
- Require a sensible baseline plus at least three alternatives and a justified validation protocol. Distinguish planned comparisons from measured results.
- Final group requirements include a three-minute YouTube demo, report with notebook/code, dataset/data dictionary, decision logs and comparison. A sharing restriction requires a permissible dataset/access handover, not omission of the requirement.
- Each final individual submission is a one-A4 Personal Learning Journey. Do not fabricate contributions or generate assessed individual reflection reports. Factual activity logs follow the [AI-usage rule](ai-usage.md) and do not replace those deliverables.
- Bonus evidence requires named context/decision/value, early approved and manageable scope, verifiable meaningful real-world data, acquisition/dictionary/privacy handling, and a practical recommendation. Online data alone earns no automatic bonus.
- Declare actual AI assistance and verification truthfully. A proposal-stage declaration does not cover later review automatically. Report incomplete evidence as pending; do not estimate awarded marks.
