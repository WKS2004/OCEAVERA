The current table preserves all 23,934 rows and the exact parsed values of 22 retained columns, in source order. This stage removes columns only; it does not filter rows, impute values, normalize coordinates, select species or join environmental data. Its checksum and counts are in the [column-screening record](2026-10-10-obis-area-230-column-screening.md). Earlier row-filter findings above are historical and are not applied to the current output.

Publisher quality flags are retained as source evidence. In particular, `ON_LAND` is not removed by this pass because actual spatial matching to the selected environmental grid and marine domain has not yet been implemented; the flag is not silently treated as a confirmed location error. All observed counts describe this fixed Area 230 snapshot only and do not establish a final modelling population.
